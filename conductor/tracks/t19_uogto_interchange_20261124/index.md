This track resumes the [deferred interchange proposal](https://github.com/edithatogo/paignia/pull/36)
with the public UOGTO reproduction case as its first consumer. It supersedes the
earlier deferral for this bounded case.


The [term coverage and native replay](../../../verification/uogto_interchange/README.md)
exercise the entire pinned vocabulary and the bounded backward-induction,
perfect-recall and normal-form profiles. The CI artifact contains the term
evidence, numerical results and installed static wire receipt.
