## Runner qualification

Prefer an official GitHub Actions just-in-time runner in a single-use Apple
Container for verified, same-repository revisions. Bind its label and
acceptance receipt to the exact workflow run, attempt, head and job. Keep
the original test and scanner oracles, process the public contribution path
under its existing isolation, and remove the runner after its assigned job.
[GitHub self-hosted runner reference](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/runners/self-hosted-runners)

Paignia already has a qualified official runner route for
[Offline AI Security](https://github.com/edithatogo/paignia/actions/runs/45612677924).
Promotion of other jobs requires the original audit to record real coverage
on the selected runtime. Public Paignia jobs that use StepSecurity's
GitHub-hosted audit retain that qualified placement pending an equivalent
verified control. Benchmark measurements require a physical match for their
declared architecture. Rosetta can qualify compatible x64 functional checks.
[Harden-Runner deployment support](https://github.com/step-security/harden-runner#-feature-availability)
