## Shared scientific contract, without duplicated implementations

Reuse the linked refinement's tests. In particular, preserve lawful player
observations and legal-action masks; analyst draws must not reveal hidden state.
Keep parameter/model/equilibrium/measurement/replicate/solver identities distinct;
labels do not imply independence. Solution concept, catalogue coverage, selection,
solver version/status/residuals and admitted scope survive serialization. Solver
failure is not economic zero; restart frequencies are not behavioural probabilities.
Behavioural learning/noisy-choice assumptions are not renamed Nash predictions.

Keep signed values, actor-payoff versus welfare assumptions and transfer/resource
accounting explicit. The shared noise control has one parameter state and two
replicates A=[-1,1], B=[1,-1]: parameter-conditional means and parameter EVPI are zero;
naively treating replicates as learnable states gives one. Voiage owns that arithmetic;
producers own truthful identities and valuation. Declare a different estimand if
future process outcomes are observed before action. Existing eight SI case IDs
remain unchanged; this is a negative control within their identity/VOI tests.

Solver exploration traces are not causal play traces. Selected-policy/native
execution requires accepted time/state/RNG/replay contracts; solver-budget changes
must not consume unrelated exogenous randomness. Seed equality alone is not pairing.
Preserve full UOGTO IRIs, legacy mappings and actual source-specific licences;
reject collisions/unknowns, and do not fork ontology assets into the solver.
Chance-zero, missing-payoff and tie semantics differ between existing seeds and
runtime constructors; map an explicit admitted profile rather than silently normalise.
